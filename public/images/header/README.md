## Sky prompt

Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: a separate panoramic sky illustration for the TOP navigation header of a website. The website footer already has a ground-level golden valley landscape; this header must represent the sky ABOVE that world, not another landscape.
Primary request: a soft hand-painted open sky viewed looking upward, warm pale cream sunlight in the center, delicate muted powder-blue color toward the upper corners, airy peach and ivory clouds gathered near the upper outer edges. The scene begins at the top of the page and naturally opens downward into a warm off-white page.
Style/medium: sophisticated cartoony gouache animation background art, visible light dry-brush strokes and soft paper texture, simple organic painted shapes. Match the warmth of a golden-hour storybook landscape without depicting land.
Composition/framing: extremely wide panoramic 3:1 canvas. Keep the center 75 percent very pale and uncluttered for a website logo and navigation. Cloud details confined to the outer edges, with an open patch of pale sky in the middle. Top-to-bottom composition: powder-blue and cream open sky above, thinning pale clouds toward the sides, bottom softly fading to warm off-white #fafaf8. This is a shallow atmospheric strip, not a hero illustration.
Constraints: NO ground, hills, mountains, horizon, grass, valley, road, buildings, birds, people, sun disk, foliage, lettering, UI, logos, border or watermark. No sunset landscape. The canvas shows ONLY sky, painted clouds and atmospheric color. Do not recreate the footer scene.

## Canopy prompt

Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: a transparent tree-canopy layer hanging from an upper corner of a small website header. The header is open sky; the footer depicts the ground below, so this branch must originate from ABOVE.
Primary request: one delicate, sparse leafy tree branch entering from the upper right corner and curving gently downward and toward the left. The branch attaches to the tree outside the TOP of the canvas; its small leaves hang downward. This is an overhead canopy, not a plant growing from the ground.
Style/medium: sophisticated cartoony gouache animation background art, expressive dry-brush edges and visible light paint texture. Muted sage-green and olive leaves with warm ochre highlights, a thin soft brown branch. Airy and graceful.
Composition: a sparse hanging branch, upper-right attachment point, leaves lightly distributed along a thin curving branch, large open transparent spaces between leaves. Fully visible leaf tips. Roughly square canvas with transparent padding.
Constraints: real transparent background with alpha. Only one upper-corner branch, no roots, ground, grass, pot, flowers, fruit, tree trunk, sky, landscape, horizon, birds, lettering or other objects. Do not create a dense bush. Intended to gently sway from its attachment at the top right.

## Cloud prompt

Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: a separate transparent moving cloud layer for an open-sky painted website header.
Primary request: one small airy cloud viewed looking up, cool ivory with very pale powder-blue and soft warm cream painted shading. Broad delicate hand-painted gouache shapes, softly irregular dry-brush edges. Softer and cooler than a golden ground-level sunset cloud.
Composition: one wide, slender floating cloud, centered, fully visible, wide canvas with transparent padding.
Constraints: real transparent background with alpha, no sky background, ground, foliage, sun, birds, text, border or other objects. Only one airy painted cloud.
