# Painted section illustrations

Four additional illustrations generated with the built-in imagegen tool. Their brushwork, warm cream/ochre/terracotta colors, and sage accents match the painted hero, footer, and existing card art. All four have genuine transparency and are saved as 960 × 640 WebP images (quality 84, full-quality alpha). They sit beside the explanatory copy and scale down on mobile.

## Assets and placement

- [install-toolkit.webp](install-toolkit.webp): homepage Install section; getting the core tools ready and connecting components.
- [familiar-wheel.webp](familiar-wheel.webp): Why Magewire, “The wheel was already round”; adapting an existing, proven design.
- [compiler-workshop.webp](compiler-workshop.webp): Compiler page, “Why it exists”; expressive directives becoming ordinary code.
- [fragment-boundary.webp](fragment-boundary.webp): Fragments page, “Why it exists”; a deliberate boundary around selected rendered output.

## Shared generation prompt

```text
Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: a compact standalone illustration for a section of the Magewire website.
Style/medium: sophisticated cartoony hand-painted gouache animation background art, matte brushed pigment with visible dry-brush marks, subtly uneven painted contours, soft layered shadows, delicate paper texture within the painted objects. The site already has a pale blue/ivory painted sky, a golden ochre/sage valley footer, and technical illustrations painted in warm cream, terracotta orange, charcoal, sage green and restrained lavender. Match that cohesive style. This should look painted by hand rather than like a glossy 3D toy or a flat vector icon.
Color palette: warm ivory #fafaf8, terracotta orange, golden ochre, charcoal, muted sage green, and a little lavender. Restrained soft warm light.
Composition/framing: landscape 3:2 canvas. One clear, compact scene in the central 80 percent, balanced with generous transparent padding around the full silhouette. Keep every meaningful object fully visible. Strong, legible shapes at a rendered width of 280 to 400 pixels. Ground only the objects with a very faint painted shadow, no base pedestal or backdrop.
Constraints: real transparent background with alpha. No words, labels, logo, watermark, border, opaque background, clouds, birds, extra scenery or people. Simple angle-bracket/code punctuation is allowed only when the subject below calls for it. No neon cables, shiny plastic, polished glass, CGI lighting, thick black comic outlines or realistic product photography. Output one finished standalone illustration.
```

## Subject prompts

### install-toolkit

```text
Primary request: an open terracotta-orange developer's toolkit, containing two cream template cards (one with a simple large </> code symbol, the other with an orange toggle and charcoal lines), a few matching interlocking component blocks laid beside it, and a small sage-green completion check tag. The image conveys getting the core tools ready and connecting the pieces of a Magento interface. A small crafted toolbox/workbench vignette, no person, no labels, no actual program text.
```

### familiar-wheel

```text
Primary request: one beautifully made, already complete round wooden wheel with warm ochre rim and sage-green spokes, resting beside a simple terracotta-orange frame with a round matching axle fitting. A few quiet cream component blocks complete the little workshop scene. The complete wheel and compatible fitting make a clear visual metaphor for reusing a proven design and adapting it rather than reinventing it. The wheel is round, mechanically plausible, and the dominant shape. No vehicles, buildings, extra tools, text or logos.
```

### compiler-workshop

```text
Primary request: a gentle little template workshop: a cream document card with one large terracotta @ directive symbol enters a pair of simple ochre printing rollers; a matching cream card with a large charcoal </> code symbol emerges on the other side. Small painted path marks connect the input, workshop and output. The equipment is compact and friendly, the two documents are clearly related. This is an editorial metaphor for converting expressive template directives into ordinary code, not a detailed technical diagram. No real program text, captions, numbers or logos.
```

### fragment-boundary

```text
Primary request: one cream browser window with a thin charcoal top bar. Within it a warm terracotta painted rectangular boundary neatly frames a small group of markup/content cards: one with simple angle brackets, one with curly braces, and one with lavender style swatches. Surrounding page content remains lightly indicated in cream and gray outside the boundary. A small sage-green check stamp sits at the edge of the marked region. This is a visual metaphor for a deliberate typed boundary around a selected piece of rendered output. The framed selected region should be the focal point. No real program text, labels, lock/shield, logos or floating extra windows.
```
