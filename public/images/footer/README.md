# Painted footer artwork

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool. The landscape, transparent cloud, and foreground foliage are saved here as optimized WebP assets. CSS animates the clouds and foliage independently and adds drifting fireflies; footer links remain HTML.

The homepage hero has [separate sky, cloud, and canopy artwork](../header/README.md) behind its headline. The hero and footer share pause and reduced-motion controls.

- Landscape: [landscape.webp](landscape.webp)
- Transparent cloud: [cloud.webp](cloud.webp)
- Transparent foreground foliage: [foliage.webp](foliage.webp)

## Landscape prompt

Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: panoramic illustrated backdrop for the MagewirePHP website footer, with real HTML links layered above it.
Primary request: a charming cartoony hand-painted landscape, a warm quiet valley at golden hour, painterly animation background aesthetic.
Style/medium: beautiful gouache on lightly textured paper, visible broad dry-brush marks, soft irregular hand-painted edges, simple organic shapes, sophisticated editorial storybook illustration. Clearly painted, not vector, not photorealistic, not 3D.
Composition/framing: very wide panoramic 3:1 landscape. Upper 55 percent is a pale warm cream-peach sky with plenty of clean negative space for dark webpage text and buttons. Landscape begins just below the middle. Layered distant muted terracotta mountains, rolling sage green and ochre hills, a small winding pale path through the valley. Dark forest green leafy grasses frame the bottom corners, the central valley stays open. A small soft golden sun near the horizon, understated wispy painted clouds far to the sides. A calm inviting sense of depth.
Color palette: warm off-white #fafaf8 and pale peach sky, burnt orange #f26322 accents in the hills, muted sage green, ochre, rich deep green foreground. Gentle warm light.
Constraints: image fills the entire canvas, panoramic composition, top center must be light and uncluttered for a footer interface. No text, lettering, logos, buttons, border, frame, watermark, people, buildings, devices or UI.

## Cloud prompt

Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: a transparent cloud layer to animate over a hand-painted website footer landscape.
Primary request: one single wide, soft, wispy cartoon cloud painted in warm ivory and very pale peach gouache. Horizontal, organic rounded shapes with expressive soft dry-brush edges and gentle paper-like paint texture, an animation background aesthetic. Simplified and painterly, matching a warm golden-hour storybook landscape.
Composition/framing: one wide slender cloud wisp, centered in a wide canvas, fully visible with space around all edges.
Constraints: genuinely transparent background with alpha, no sky, landscape, scenery, text, border, shadow, UI, or other objects. Only one painted cloud. Keep the cloud airy and pale.

## Foliage prompt

Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: transparent foreground foliage layer for an animated hand-painted landscape website footer.
Primary request: one small cluster of windswept meadow grasses and leafy plants, rooted along the bottom edge, with tall slender bending stems and a few ochre grass seed heads.
Style/medium: cartoony gouache animation background art, broad visible dry-brush marks, irregular organic painted edges. Match a warm golden-hour valley with muted sage hills and deep forest-green foreground. Simplified hand-painted shapes with graceful clear silhouettes.
Composition: isolated full cluster seen from the front, roughly square canvas, taller stems toward the middle, shorter leaves at the sides. All tips and leaves fully visible, roots converge near the bottom center. Leave transparent padding around the top and sides.
Color palette: dark forest green #233c2e, sage #637352, a few warm golden ochre accents. Warm rim light.
Constraints: real transparent background with alpha, only this one foliage cluster, no landscape, sky, ground, pot, flowers, people, text, border or other objects. Intended to sway as a separate animated layer.
